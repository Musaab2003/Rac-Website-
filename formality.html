Ceremonial Formalities

“The Guiding Principles, Invocations, Anthems, and Pledges of Rotaract ICBT”

The Heritage of Formality

Solemnity • Respect • Unity

In the Rotaract movement, formalities are not mere rituals; they are the solemn pillars that anchor our brotherhood, instill discipline, and honor our shared oath of "Fellowship Through Service." From the resonance of the Rotaract Invocation to the respect accorded to the National Flag, every formality connects our club with Rotary International District 3220 and collegiate youth across the globe.

Ethical Constellation

The Four-Way Test

Of the things we think, say, or do — the universal moral beacon guiding every Rotaractor.

Pillar I

Is it the TRUTH?

Upholding absolute honesty, transparency, and intellectual integrity in all personal, academic, and community endeavors.

Pillar II

Is it FAIR to all?

Treating all members, partners, and beneficiaries with equitable respect, mutual dignity, and impartial justice.

Pillar III

Build GOODWILL & Friendships?

Fostering genuine brotherhood, collaborative kinship, and lifelong bonds that bridge across divides and differences.

Pillar IV

BENEFICIAL to all?

Ensuring that every project and decision creates sustainable value and uplifting outcomes for the entire community.

Words of Reflection

The Rotaract Invocation

Recited at the opening of official gatherings to seek wisdom, humility, and selfless purpose.

R

Remember us always as thy children,

O

Oh Lord, instill in us the true meaning of friendship,

T

That the differences of cultures and creed should not matter,

A

At all times endow us with the Desire to serve,

R

Remembering that we too often need help,

A

As and when the need for service arises,

C

Caring to make our badge of Rotaract worthwhile,

T

Tell us that we have not lived in vain.

Patriotic Devotion

National Flag Salutation

An oath of allegiance to Sri Lanka, rendered in our motherland's national tongues.

Official English Translation

“I, salute the National Flag of Sri Lanka which signifies the Independence of my Country and stands for all its People united as one Nation. I pledge all allegiance to Sri Lanka and undertake to work towards its Peace and Prosperity in a spirit of Tolerance and Love.”
