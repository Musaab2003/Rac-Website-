Awards & Achievements

Honoring our members and initiatives that have achieved distinction across Rotary International District 3220.

Best RYLArian of the Year 2025

The Rotaract Club of ICBT is proud to celebrate Rtr. Dinya Himasari's incredible achievement as the Best RYLArian of the Year (Female) at the Rotary Youth Leadership Awards 2025!

Aspirational Leader of the Year 2023–2024

Rtr. Yasodara Dunuwille Koralege has achieved remarkable success at the 34th Rotaract District Assembly, securing this prestigious district award.

Gold Award for Most Outstanding Initiative with the Rotary Family

Recognized for the outstanding joint initiative “Reel for a Cause” executed in harmony with our sponsoring Rotary club.

Silver Distinction • Rotaract District Citation

Conferred for exemplary club administration, continuous community presence, and high-standard district project reporting.

Awarded to Rtr. Yasodara Dunuwille Koralege in recognition of diligent recordkeeping, timely dispatches, and administrative excellence.

Spirit of Service Award

Bestowed upon Rtr. Yasodara Dunuwille Koralege for unwavering dedication and selfless commitment to grassroots humanitarian efforts.

Award of Appreciation
