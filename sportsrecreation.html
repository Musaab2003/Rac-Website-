Athletic Passion, Well-Being & Healthy Competition

What is Sports & Recreation?

Sports and Recreation Avenue champions physical fitness, athletic discipline, mental wellbeing, and vibrant camaraderie among club members. Through competitive tournaments, outdoor expeditions, and wellness challenges, we unite collegiate youth in the joyful spirit of healthy competition.

Strategic Pillars

Key Activities & Operations

Core avenues of action through which we mobilize members and deliver impactful experiences.

Inter-Club Sports Tournaments

Organizing cricket championships, futsal tournaments, badminton leagues, and athletics meets.

Fitness & Wellness Challenges

Promoting holistic physical wellness through marathon relays, functional fitness bootcamps, and yoga sessions.

Recreational Expeditions & Outings

Curating team hiking trips, beach sports gatherings, and adventure retreats that forge lifelong bonds.
