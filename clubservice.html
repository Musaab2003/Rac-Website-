What is Club Service?

Club Service Avenue focuses on the internal development, unity, and effective functioning of the Rotaract Club of ICBT. It ensures that all members feel connected, motivated, and inspired to contribute to the club’s vision. It is the sacred avenue that strengthens fellowship among members and promotes effective club operations through meetings, events, and team-building retreats.
