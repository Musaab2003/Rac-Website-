Timeline of Milestones

The Greek Chronicle

2018 – 2019 • Charter

Consecration of the Club

Under charter president Rtr. Shameen Samarawickrema and sponsored by Rotary Club of Cinnamon Gardens.

2019 – 2020 • Ascendance

District Emergence

Under Rtr. PP. Jehaad Kitchilan, recognized among best emerging clubs with Golden Award for Best Secretary.

2020 – 2021 • Fortitude

Virtual Conquests & PETS/SETS

Rtr. PP. Basharath Isamath steered virtual programs and hosted PETS & SETS 2020.

2021 – 2022 • Inclusion

District Citation Honors

Rtr. PP. Tashya Wickramarachchi champions inclusivity and wins the Bronze District Citation.

2022 – 2023 • Transformation

Community Impact Growth

Led by Rtr. PP. Aqeelah Basheer with transformative community and youth initiatives.

2023 – 2024 • 36 Quests

Korean Fusion & UGRAM Gold

Rtr. PP. Umar Shafeek leads 36 successful projects and earns multiple district awards.
